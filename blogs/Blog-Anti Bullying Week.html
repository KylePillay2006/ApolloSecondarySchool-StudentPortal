Annual Sports Day 2026: A Day of Fun, Fitness, and School Spirit

The Apollo Secondary School Annual Sports Day 2026 was a resounding success, bringing together students, teachers, and parents for a day filled with excitement, camaraderie, and healthy competition. Held on March 15, 2026, at the school's state-of-the-art sports complex, the event showcased the incredible athletic talent and school spirit that defines our institution.

The day kicked off with a vibrant opening ceremony featuring a marching band performance and a colorful parade of houses. Students competed in a wide range of events including track races, relay races, long jump, high jump, shot put, and javelin. The highlight of the day was the highly anticipated inter-house relay race, which had the entire crowd on their feet cheering loudly. Our learners demonstrated not only their physical abilities but also their resilience, sportsmanship, and teamwork.

Watch the Sports Day 2026 Highlights

The event concluded with an inspiring awards ceremony where outstanding athletes were recognized for their achievements. Trophies and medals were awarded to individual winners and the overall winning house. The Sports Day not only promoted physical fitness but also strengthened the bonds within our school community. A huge thank you to all the teachers, staff, parents, and especially the learners who made this day an unforgettable experience. We are already looking forward to next year's event!
