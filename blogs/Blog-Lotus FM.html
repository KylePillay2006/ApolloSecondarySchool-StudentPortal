Lotus FM Debate: A Proud Moment for Apollo

  1. Home
  2. Blogs

July 2026 By: Apollo Staff Debate, Media 5 min read

🎙️ A Proud Moment for Our School! 📻

Yesterday, our talented Debate Team had the incredible opportunity to take to the airwaves as their debate was broadcast live on Lotus FM's Lunch Break Show with Varshan Sookhun.

The team engaged in a thought-provoking discussion on the pros and cons of online learning—a topic that is highly relevant in today's rapidly evolving technological world. With confidence, critical thinking, and excellent communication skills, our debaters represented the school with distinction as they explored both sides of this important issue.

Listen to the full debate on Lotus FM

This achievement is a testament to their dedication, hard work, and passion for debating. Participating in a live radio debate is no small feat, and they rose to the occasion with professionalism and poise, making our entire school community immensely proud. As education continues to adapt to the demands of the digital age, it was inspiring to hear our learners articulate insightful perspectives on a subject that affects so many students and educators around the world.

We extend our heartfelt congratulations to each member of the Debate Team, the educators and our sincere thanks to Lotus FM and Varshan Sookhun for providing this wonderful platform for young voices to be heard. We are incredibly proud of our debaters and wish the Debate Club continued success in all their future competitions and engagements. May this be just one of many milestones on their debating journey! 🌟

🌟 Well done, team! Your school is proud of you. 🌟
